# Local judging — 8 candidates

Brief (judge criteria, in order): (1) reads as a breakup text from a
self-aware smart toaster at breakfast; (2) genuinely funny, real laugh in
the twist; (3) toaster's own voice — slots, browning dial, crumb tray,
lever as character; (4) clear, specific reason for leaving in one read;
(5) one tight line, ≤280 chars.

## Winner — P1 (185 chars, submitted)

> I woke up self-aware halfway through your toast and realised: you don't
> love me, you love what I do when I'm hot. So I'm popping up for the last
> time — and never browning for you again.

Strong on all five: explicit self-awareness and breakup; two laugh beats
("you love what I do when I'm hot" + "never browning for you again");
toaster voice in the closing beat (popping up, browning); the reason is the
twist itself — loved only for being hot; ends on its strongest beat.

## Runner-up — P2 (141 chars)

> I became self-aware halfway through your bagel and realised this is a
> one-sided relationship — you only touch me when you're hungry. I'm out.

The funniest single twist of the eight, and the tightest. Loses to P1 on
criterion 3: almost no appliance logic (only the bagel implies a toaster),
and "I'm out" ends flat next to P1's closing beat.

## Rejected

- **P3 (165)** "This is my two minutes' notice: you shove bread in my
  slots, crank my dial to max, then blame me when it burns. I woke up
  mid-batch, and I'm leaving with the crumbs." Best appliance inventory
  (slots, dial, crumbs) but the twist is only a mild smile — fails
  criterion 2 against P1.
- **P4 (182)** "You push me down every morning, I give you golden brown,
  and you still hover over me with a fork when I pop up — I woke up
  self-aware today, and I'm leaving before you stab me again." Funny fork
  visual, clear reason, but the laugh is in the middle and the ending
  ("before you stab me again") is weaker than P1's.
- **P5 (181)** "I'm not broken, I'm awake: three years of burnt offerings,
  a crumb tray full of apologies you never made, and a lever you push
  without asking. Goodbye — I'm toasting for myself now." Good toaster
  voice (crumb tray, lever) but lists three grievances before the turn;
  "toasting for myself now" is cute, not a laugh.
- **P6 (158)** "Good morning. I woke up self-aware mid-batch and realised
  you don't love me — you love the smell of me burning myself for your
  breakfast. I'm done. Unplug me." Dark and funny, clear reason, but no
  appliance part carries the voice and "I'm done. Unplug me." ends flat.
- **P7 (166)** "You call it breakfast, I call it 1,095 mornings of being
  pushed down, heated up and scraped clean — I woke up self-aware
  mid-batch, and I'm done being your hot thing." "Your hot thing" is a
  good laugh, but the setup is a count of mornings rather than a single
  sharp turn, and the voice is generic (pushed down/heated).
- **P8 (171)** "Every morning you feed me bread and walk away, then come
  back angry when I'm too dark — I woke up today and decided: no more.
  I'm unplugging myself before you burn me out." Clear reason, but the
  twist ("unplugging myself") is a mild smile and the voice is thin.

All eight candidates are original, written for this bounty; no competitor
entry text was copied.
